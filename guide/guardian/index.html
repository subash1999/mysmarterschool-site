Getting started / Guardian

The guardian guide.

Find linked student records, attendance and examination results.

1. Open the guardian dashboard

  1. Use the application sign-in address and account supplied by the school.
  2. On Available Dashboards, choose Guardian for the intended school.
  3. Select the relevant School Session and open My Students to find the linked student records.
Missing a student?Ask the school admin to check the guardian–student relationship on the student record.

2. Find an examination result

  1. Open Exam Groups and choose the relevant examination group.
  2. Open the grade’s marks view and find the student result.
  3. Review the displayed subject marks and result summary. The table can show percentage or grade information.
  4. For a result question or missing examination, contact the school to confirm the selected session and publication details.

3. Check attendance and dates

  1. Open My Students Attendance to review available attendance records.
  2. Use School Calendar to find school events and dates.
  3. For a correction to student details or records, contact the school office.
