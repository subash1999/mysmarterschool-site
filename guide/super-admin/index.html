Create the school

  1. Sign in and select Super Admin on Available Dashboards.
  2. Open Schools and use Create School. Enter the school details and review them before saving.
  3. Open the saved school record to check its name, address and contact details.

Add school admin access

  1. Open School Admins and create the school administrator record.
  2. Associate the administrator with the intended school and user account.
  3. Ask the administrator to select the School Admin dashboard, then follow the school setup guide.
Role selection mattersA user can hold more than one role. Confirm both the role and school before starting a task.
