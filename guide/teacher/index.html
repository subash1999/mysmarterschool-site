Getting started / Teacher

The teacher guide.

Record class attendance and enter marks for your subjects.

1. Choose your workspace

  1. Sign in, then select Teacher for your school on Available Dashboards.
  2. Choose the intended School Session in the sidebar.
  3. Check the subjects assigned to you. Ask your school admin to correct a missing assignment.

2. Take and review attendance

  1. Open Student Attendance and start a new attendance search.
  2. Choose the school session, grade, subject and date. Use a date that is today or earlier.
  3. Open the attendance entry form. Mark the students who are present and save attendance.
  4. Return to the same class and date to view or edit the record.
Check the class and date firstThese screenshots show subject-based attendance. The available attendance workflow depends on the school’s configuration.

3. Enter examination marks

  1. Open Exam Groups, then the relevant examination group and grade.
  2. Choose the marks editing action for your assigned subjects.
  3. Enter the marks obtained for each student. Use the absent option when the student did not sit the examination.
  4. Save the marks, then review the marks table before finishing.

Examination groups and their grade and subject setup are managed by the school admin. Use the school’s agreed marking rules when entering values.
