About Smart School

Built around the
work of a school.

A school management system for Nepal, bringing student records, classroom workflows and fee billing into one product.

From Nepal.
For the school day.

Built and maintained
by Subash Niroula.

Smart School brings together school administration, teachers, accountants and guardians. Its role-based dashboards cover student records, grades and subjects, examinations and results, attendance, calendar events and fee billing.

Learn more about Subash and his work at subashn.com.np.

See the work before the pitch.

The screenshots throughout this site come from the actual application running locally with generated records. Demo Academy is fictional. Student names, marks and billing amounts are illustrative.

The getting-started guides walk through the workflows shown in those screenshots.
